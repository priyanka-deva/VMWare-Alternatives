**Classification:** Internal use  
**Basis:** Both user-supplied OT lists, with validated research mapped back to the original entries.

## What this roster shows

Every one of the **97 detailed supplier/application entries** in “OT and VMware.md” is retained, in its original category and order. All **65 entries** in “OT Applications.md” are cross-referenced below. These are source entries, not 97 distinct applications or independently verified internal deployments. Original vendor/product text is preserved; possible naming errors are not silently corrected.

**How to identify gaps:** Read the status column. A published supported configuration, conditional support and a deployment example are different outcomes. “Public support not established” means the priority research did not validate sufficient public evidence. **“Not yet researched” and “software/component identity needed” are unfinished research, not evidence that no alternative is supported.** No entry has been classified as universally unsupported.

The previous report covered a priority subset. This roster makes the remaining coverage visible rather than omitting suppliers or implying they were all researched.

## Reading the updated roster

All 97 original entries remain. VMware has been added without assuming support from installed use. Findings from the latest 65-group research are reconciled below. Limited-scope, conditional, identity and access gaps remain visible; there is no portfolio-wide compatibility percentage.
