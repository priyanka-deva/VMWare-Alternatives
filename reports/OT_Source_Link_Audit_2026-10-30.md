Failed routes received repeat attempts; searches and original publisher links were used for replacements. ## How to read the individual results

- **OPEN_MATCH:** destination opened and matched the cited document/product/topic; not a compatibility certificate.
- Other **OPEN_...** results qualify that success: portal, download landing page, index, community context, related product, version uncertainty, freshness caution or third-party mirror.
- **NOT_OPEN_404 / BLOCKED_403 / TIMEOUT:** observed retrieval failure, not proof of incompatibility.
- **READER_SIZE_LIMIT:** body could not be inspected with this reader; URL validity remains unresolved.
- Canonical destination is shown only when a redirect was actually returned; otherwise it repeats the original URL.
