01Application hosting

Prioritize specific, supportable configurations

DeltaV has a defined Hyper-V path. FactoryTalk has conditional VMware / Hyper-V guidance. Siemens support varies by product and component. These findings establish options within stated boundaries.

Leadership implicationBuild a qualification shortlist around the installed software, release and node role. Supplier-level support cannot approve a complete OEM system.

02Backup & recovery

Protection coverage is a separate platform decision

Cohesity–VMware is published backup interoperability. Cohesity HPE VM Essentials protection is guest-agent based; its Proxmox evidence is a repository / Remote Adapter workflow. Commvault and Acronis have their own version and method restrictions.

Leadership implicationQualify the protection method and recovery path independently of the application vendor’s hosting approval.

03Qualification priorities

Distinguish inventory context from software identity

Of the 47 former I entries, 43 are supplier, equipment or mixed records requiring workload mapping. Four are software names/aliases with product mapping pending. Cognex has a separate component-specific VM exclusion and unresolved blueprint conflict.

Leadership implicationAssociate supplier and equipment records with their actual software and node roles before assessing compatibility. Obtain the product mapping for the four software-identity cases.

04Enterprise scope

Separate provider-hosted cores from customer VMs

The register identifies hosted cores for Signals Notebook, SuccessFactors Learning and Veeva Vault. Internal product mappings and customer-operated connectors or integration nodes still need confirmation.

Leadership implicationKeep hosted cores separate from customer hypervisor selection. Scope any local components as distinct workloads.

Start with known software identities and a defined target configuration, then resolve OEM, release, recovery and internal quality approvals before an installed-system decision.

Read the classifications and platform boundaries

Azure Local is separate from Hyper-V. Nutanix means AHV only in this matrix; Nutanix branding alone does not prove AHV. HPE refers to the HVM hypervisor. Oracle refers to OLVM / Oracle Linux KVM. Canonical MicroCloud / OpenStack requires the exact distribution to match. Citrix XenServer support does not establish XCP-ng support.

Named or conditional support still requires an installed-release and configuration match. The source register retains access limitations, historical evidence and document-identity conflicts. It does not certify the installed estate.
